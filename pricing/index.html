What Custom Managed includes — and what it does not

The managed platform fee includes ongoing platform management, support, monitoring and an agreed allocation of development and configuration capacity. Major new functionality, substantial integrations, large-scale data preparation and services outside the agreed monthly allocation are quoted separately.

Catalogue import, manual product loading, and content administration are covered under Nexus Data & Catalogue Services — not unlimited inside the monthly managed fee.

Nexus Data & Catalogue Services

Product data is a separate service

Platform subscriptions cover the software and managed technology. Preparing, cleansing, and loading catalogues is scoped as its own Nexus service — so a large product list does not silently expand your monthly fee.
